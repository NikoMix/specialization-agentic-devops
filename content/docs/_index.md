The toolkit is organised into four parts.

**Pre-audit** — [Overview](/docs/overview/), [Pre-Qualification Requirements](/docs/requirements/) and the [Audit Process](/docs/audit-process/) explain what the specialization is, what you must hold before requesting an audit, and how the audit itself runs.

**Controls** — [Module A](/docs/module-a/) covers the seven general organisational controls shared by every Advanced Specialization. [Module B](/docs/module-b/) covers the six Agentic DevOps-specific controls. Every control page carries an evidence checklist, guidance, an evidence status table and a common-gaps table.

**Delivery** — the [Engagement Playbook](/docs/engagement/) productises the offering: qualification, discovery workshop, Well-Architected assessment, reference architectures and the customer deliverable templates.

**Contribution** — [Innersource](/docs/innersource/) is the model that keeps this toolkit current: every delivered engagement feeds lessons back through issues and pull requests.
