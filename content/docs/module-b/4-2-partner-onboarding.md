## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has a **reusable customer onboarding pack** plus the delivery templates required to ship Agentic DevOps engagements consistently.

**Typical questions:**

- Do you have a standard onboarding pack you give every new customer?
- Are HLD / LLD / runbook / KT plan / hypercare plan templates documented and reusable?
- Have these templates been used on a real engagement?

## Evidence Guidance

### Customer Onboarding Pack

A 5–10 slide deck or short Word document covering:

- Engagement overview and timeline
- Roles and responsibilities (RACI)
- Communication plan (cadence, channels, escalation contacts)
- Security and access plan (how the consultant will access the customer's GitHub / Azure environment)
- Deliverables list with dates
- Definition of done

Reuse the [Offering One-Pager](/docs/engagement/offering-one-pager/) as the front cover.

### Delivery Templates

Each template is a starting structure — not a one-size-fits-all document. The auditor wants to see that templates are reused and adapted, not that every engagement gets an identical Word file.

For each template, capture:

- Owner (who maintains it)
- Version history
- One real-engagement instance (anonymised)

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Templates exist but no real-engagement example | Anonymise the most recent engagement's HLD or runbook |
| Onboarding pack lives only in someone's head | Capture in a slide deck — 5 slides is enough for the auditor |
| Templates are inconsistent in style | Adopt the ones in `static/templates/deliverables/` as the baseline |
| KT plan and hypercare plan missing | Use the downloadable Word templates as a starting point |
