This toolkit is only useful if it stays current. The innersource model makes contribution part of the engagement, not an afterthought.

| Page | Read it when |
|---|---|
| [Contributing](/docs/innersource/contributing/) | You are about to open your first pull request |
| [Content Governance](/docs/innersource/content-governance/) | You need to know who reviews what, and to which standard |
| [Roadmap & Backlog](/docs/innersource/roadmap/) | You are looking for something useful to pick up |

Three issue templates drive the loop: `lesson-learned` after every engagement, `control-improvement` when audit guidance turns out to be wrong or incomplete, and `template-improvement` when a customer deliverable template needs work.
