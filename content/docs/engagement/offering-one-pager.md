## When to use this

Use the offering one-pager at the **qualify** phase, when a Microsoft seller, PDM, or warm customer contact asks "what does your Agentic DevOps offering actually do?" One page, ten minutes to read, suitable for forwarding to a CTO or platform lead.

## Inputs you need from the customer

| # | Input | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Industry + size | Public profile / LinkedIn | Free text |
| 2 | Primary SCM today (GitHub, ADO, Bitbucket, GitLab) | Quick call or LinkedIn | Dropdown |
| 3 | Existing GitHub Copilot adoption (none / pilot / org-wide) | Quick call | Dropdown |
| 4 | Top business outcome (cycle time, security, talent retention, cost) | Quick call | Dropdown |

## Step-by-step

The following sequence produces a customer-ready one-pager in under 60 minutes.

1. Download the PPTX one-pager template.
2. Replace the customer logo, sector, and top-line outcome on slide 1.
3. Adjust the deliverables list to match the engagement length (6 vs. 12 weeks).
4. Update the indicative price band based on team size.
5. Save as PDF and share with the Microsoft seller / customer contact.

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

A single-slide PDF with: offering name, outcomes, scope (in / out), deliverables, prerequisites, timeline, indicative price band, your firm's qualifications and credentials.
