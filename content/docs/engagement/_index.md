The Engagement Playbook turns the specialization from an audit exercise into a **repeatable, sellable offering**. Work through it in order for a new customer; dip into individual pages when you already have an engagement in flight.

| Stage | Page | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Position | [Offering One-Pager](/docs/engagement/offering-one-pager/) | Customer-facing summary of the offering |
| 2 — Qualify | [Qualification Questionnaire](/docs/engagement/qualification-questionnaire/) | Go / no-go decision and scoping inputs |
| 3 — Discover | [Discovery Workshop Kit](/docs/engagement/discovery-workshop/) | Current state, target state, prioritised backlog |
| 4 — Assess | [WAF Assessment](/docs/engagement/waf-assessment/) | Scored Well-Architected review with recommendations |
| 5 — Feed platforms | [Assessment Platform Inputs](/docs/engagement/assessment-platform-inputs/) | Findings loaded into Microsoft assessment tooling |
| 6 — Design | [Reference Architectures](/docs/engagement/reference-architectures/) | Target architecture selected and adapted |
| 7 — Document | [Customer Deliverable Templates](/docs/engagement/deliverables/) | HLD, LLD, runbook, KT plan, hypercare plan |
| 8 — Close | [Definition of Done](/docs/engagement/definition-of-done/) | Signed-off exit criteria |

Everything produced here is dual-purpose: it delivers the customer engagement **and** becomes the anonymised evidence for [Module B control 1.1](/docs/module-b/1-1-agentic-devops-capability/).
