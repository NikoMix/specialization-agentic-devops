Five documents make up a complete Agentic DevOps engagement deliverable set. Each page explains what belongs in the document, how to fill it in, and links to a downloadable Word template.

| Deliverable | Produced during | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| [High-Level Design (HLD)](/docs/engagement/deliverables/hld-template/) | Design phase | Architects, sponsors |
| [Low-Level Design (LLD)](/docs/engagement/deliverables/lld-template/) | Design phase | Implementing engineers |
| [Runbook](/docs/engagement/deliverables/runbook-template/) | Build / scale phase | Platform operations |
| [Knowledge Transfer Plan](/docs/engagement/deliverables/kt-plan-template/) | Scale phase | Customer engineering teams |
| [Hypercare Plan](/docs/engagement/deliverables/hypercare-plan-template/) | Handover | Operations and sponsors |

Anonymised copies of these documents are accepted evidence for [Module B control 1.1](/docs/module-b/1-1-agentic-devops-capability/) and [control 4.2](/docs/module-b/4-2-partner-onboarding/).
