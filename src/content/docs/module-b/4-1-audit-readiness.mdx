## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has prepared a **structured, indexed evidence package** ready for review — not a loose collection of files emailed at the last minute.

**Typical questions:**

- Is there a master evidence index mapping every control to evidence files?
- Has the package been reviewed internally before submission?
- Are documents version-controlled and consistently named?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

The five items below cover the index, the package, the internal review, and the submission record.

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

## Evidence Guidance

### Folder Structure

Recommended layout for the submitted evidence package:

```
Audit_<Year>/
├── 00_Evidence_Index.xlsx
├── 01_Module_A/
│   ├── A1.1_Organisational_Data/
│   ├── A1.2_Financial_Documentation/
│   ├── A2.1_Service_Delivery_Methodology/
│   ├── A2.2_Quality_Management/
│   ├── A3.1_Customer_Satisfaction/
│   ├── A3.2_Complaint_Handling/
│   └── A3.3_Security_Privacy/
├── 02_Module_B/
│   ├── B1.1_Agentic_DevOps_Capability/
│   ├── B2.1_ACR_Performance/
│   ├── B2.2_Customer_Diversity/
│   ├── B3.1_Certifications/
│   ├── B4.1_Audit_Readiness/
│   └── B4.2_Partner_Onboarding/
└── 99_Auditor_Queries/
```

### File Naming

`[ControlRef]_[DocumentType]_v[N].pdf` — e.g. `A2.1_DeliveryMethodology_v2.pdf`.

### Internal Pre-Audit Review

Run a 1–2 hour internal review with:

- Practice lead (overall sign-off)
- Compliance / quality lead (Module A)
- Technical lead (Module B)
- Engagement lead (cross-reference with active customer engagements)

Document the review with a short sign-off note in the package.

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Evidence sent as loose email attachments | Repackage into the folder structure above; share via SharePoint or auditor portal |
| No master index | Build the index from the downloadable XLSX template; one row per control |
| No internal review | Block 90 minutes with practice + technical leads; document attendance and outcomes |
| File names inconsistent | Use a rename script (PowerShell `Rename-Item`) to enforce the convention |
