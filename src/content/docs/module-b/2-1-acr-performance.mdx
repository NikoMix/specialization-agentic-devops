## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation generates **$15,000 USD of Azure Consumed Revenue (ACR) per qualifying pillar**, measured over the last three months.

**Typical questions:**

- Can you show Partner Center ACR reports for the last three months?
- Are the qualifying pillars individually above the $15,000 threshold?
- Are the ACR figures associated via eligible partner types (DPOR, PAL, CSP)?

<Aside type="caution">
  ACR data in Partner Center lags by **2–4 weeks**. Confirm exact figures with your PDM, especially
  if you are close to the thresholds.
</Aside>

## How to Export ACR Data from Partner Center

The steps below are the same as for any specialization — only the pillar mapping differs.

1. Navigate to **Partner Center → Insights → Azure Revenue**
2. Set date range to the last 3 full months
3. Export to Excel / download the report
4. Filter or annotate by service to show pillar alignment
5. Highlight the total per pillar

<Aside type="tip">
  Use the **"Revenue by Customer"** view to identify unique customer contributions simultaneously —
  this saves time when preparing evidence for B.2.2 (Customer Diversity) as well.
</Aside>

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| ACR below threshold in one pillar | Discuss with PDM whether any eligible services are miscategorised; consider accelerating customer workloads |
| Association type missing for some subscriptions | Work with the customer to establish DPOR or PAL links immediately |
| ACR report doesn't show service-level breakdown | Request a detailed report from PDM or use the Insights API |
| Revenue near threshold (\$13K–\$15K) | Confirm with PDM — Partner Center may lag and actual figures may be higher |
