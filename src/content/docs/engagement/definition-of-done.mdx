## When to use this

Agree the Definition of Done at the **end of discovery**, before scope-locking the engagement. Re-confirm during hypercare exit. The DoD is the gate the customer signs to close the engagement and trigger the final invoice.

## Inputs you need from the customer

| # | Input | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signed engagement plan | Customer | PDF |
| 2 | Customer-side acceptance criteria | Customer | Free text |

## Step-by-step

The following sequence locks the DoD before delivery starts and re-uses it at engagement close.

<Steps>
1. Download the DoD template (DOCX).
2. Tailor the per-deliverable acceptance criteria to the customer's scope.
3. Review with the customer's sponsor; secure verbal agreement.
4. Both parties sign the DoD before pilot kick-off.
5. At engagement close, re-walk the DoD with the customer and capture sign-off per row.
</Steps>

## Per-deliverable acceptance criteria

| Deliverable | "Done" means |
|---|---|
| Discovery report + scorecard | Customer sponsor has reviewed and acknowledged in writing |
| HLD | Reviewed by customer architect; no open major issues |
| LLD | Includes all per-repo / per-team configuration; CODEOWNER mapping signed off |
| Pilot team enablement | Pilot team passes the success metrics for 2 consecutive sprints |
| Scale-out (3–5 teams) | All teams on the new platform; baseline metrics improved or held |
| Runbook | Tested via a dry-run incident; customer platform team can execute without partner help |
| KT plan | Customer team passes a 1-hour skills check on each module |
| Hypercare plan | 30/60/90 SLOs achieved or formally accepted with deviation notes |

## Engagement-level DoD

The engagement as a whole is done when every row below is true.

- [ ] All deliverables in the engagement plan accepted
- [ ] All success metrics either met or formally accepted with deviation notes
- [ ] All open risks / actions transferred to the customer's backlog
- [ ] Customer sponsor has signed the DoD document
- [ ] Final invoice raised
- [ ] Lessons-learned issue opened in this repo
