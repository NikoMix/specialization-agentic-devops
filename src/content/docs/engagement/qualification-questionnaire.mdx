## When to use this

After the offering one-pager lands and the customer responds with interest, run the qualification questionnaire **before** committing to a discovery workshop. It should take the customer's platform lead 20–30 minutes to fill in and gives you the signal to qualify in or out.

## Inputs you need from the customer

| # | Input | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Platform lead contact | Customer | Email |
| 2 | Org structure (number of teams / repos) | Customer | Numbers |
| 3 | Security baseline expectations | Customer | Narrative |
| 4 | AI usage policy (current) | Customer | Narrative |

## Step-by-step

The following sequence runs the qualification end-to-end.

<Steps>
1. Send the customer the downloadable Word questionnaire.
2. Give them a week. Follow up if no response in 5 business days.
3. Schedule a 30-minute scoring call once they return it.
4. Score against the rubric (below) and recommend qualify-in or qualify-out.
5. If qualify-in, share the discovery workshop kit.
</Steps>

## Questionnaire sections

The questionnaire is structured across the eight sections below.

| # | Section | Sample questions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today's SCM | What is your primary SCM? Any secondary systems? Plans to consolidate? |
| 2 | Today's CI/CD | Pipelines per team? Avg. green-build rate? Avg. PR cycle time? |
| 3 | Identity & access | Entra ID or other? EMU in use? Audit log streaming? |
| 4 | Security posture | GHAS enabled? Secret scanning findings open? Dependabot in place? |
| 5 | AI policy | Copilot allowed? Approved models? Approved data classes? |
| 6 | Developer environments | Dev Box / Codespaces in use? Local-only? Restricted? |
| 7 | Migration appetite | ADO → GitHub on the roadmap? Timeline? Owner? |
| 8 | Outcomes wanted | Top 3 business outcomes in priority order |

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

The completed questionnaire plus a one-page qualification scorecard (Go / Defer / No-go) with the rationale.

## Qualification rubric

Score each of the eight sections 0–3 (0 = blocker, 3 = ready). Sum the scores.

| Score | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | No-go — fundamental gaps, not a fit for a 6–12 week engagement |
| 9–16 | Defer — surface gaps to PDM, propose a shorter readiness sprint first |
| 17–24 | Go — proceed to discovery workshop |
